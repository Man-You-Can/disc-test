Behavioral style questionnaire

Free DISC test: how you act, communicate and make decisions

DISC describes four styles of behavior. Everyone has all four, but to different degrees. The test will show your dominant style, your strengths, your growth areas and how others can work with you best.

How it works

The test has 24 blocks of four statements, 96 in total. In each block, mark the one statement that describes you most and the one that describes you least. Answer the way you usually behave at work, not the way you would like to. Don't overthink it: your first reaction is the most accurate.

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

When you finish the test, your result will be emailed to this address. Your name, email and result are stored in the site owner's results database.

The four DISC styles

DDominance · Achiever

You move toward the goal directly and fast: you take the initiative, decide without lengthy discussion and aren't afraid of resistance. Being in control and seeing measurable results matters to you.

DirectDecisiveResults-drivenIndependentDemanding
More about the “Achiever” profile

IInfluence · Inspirer

You work through people: you make connections easily, spread enthusiasm and persuade with words and mood. Recognition and lively interaction matter to you, while formalities and routine wear you out quickly.

SociableOptimisticEmotionalPersuasiveSpontaneous
More about the “Inspirer” profile

SSteadiness · Anchor

You create stability: you keep your word, finish what you start, support people and smooth over conflicts. Predictability, trust and time to get used to change matter to you.

PatientReliableCalmAttentive to peopleConsistent
More about the “Anchor” profile

CConscientiousness · Analyst

You rely on facts and standards: you check, analyze, plan and dislike hasty decisions. Quality and being right matter to you more than speed and outward effect.

PreciseAnalyticalCautiousSystematicReserved
More about the “Analyst” profile
