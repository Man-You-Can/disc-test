Behavioral style questionnaire

Free DISC personality test: how you act, communicate and make decisions

DISC describes four behavioral styles. Everyone has all four, but to different degrees. The test will show your primary style, your strengths, your growth areas and how others can work with you best.

How it works

The test has 24 blocks of four statements, 96 in total. In each block, mark the one statement that describes you most and the one that describes you least. Answer the way you usually behave at work, not the way you would like to. Don’t overthink it: your first reaction is the most accurate.

Printable paper version: PDF test.

The four DISC styles

DDominance · Achiever

You head straight for the goal and move fast: you take the initiative, decide without lengthy discussion and aren’t afraid of pushback. Being in control and seeing measurable results matter to you.

What you’ll get

  • Your primary and secondary style and the name of your profile — one of 16
  • A chart with three lines and how strongly each style shows up, in percent
  • Your strengths, growth areas, motivation and behavior under stress
  • Tips on how best to communicate and work with you
  • A link to your report, a PDF copy and an email if you want one — no sign-up and no paid versions

How the result is calculated

In each of the 24 blocks, you mark the one statement that is most like you and the one that is least like you. Each statement belongs to one of the styles D, I, S or C. For each style, the score is the number of “most” marks minus the number of “least” marks — from −24 to +24 — and it is then converted to a percentage. The primary style is the one with the highest score; a secondary style counts if it is also clearly pronounced. Results explained →

The test in brief

How long does the test take?

About ten minutes: 24 blocks of four statements each.

Is it free?

Yes, completely: no sign-up, no ads and no paid “extended reports.”

Are there right answers?

No. Every profile is normal; answer the way you usually behave at work, not the way you’d like to.

Can a whole team take the test?

Yes: everyone takes the test via a link, and the results are collected in the admin panel.
