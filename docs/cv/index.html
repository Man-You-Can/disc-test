Why you’re not hearing back

  • The same resume for every job

    Recruiters and applicant tracking systems screen for what this specific role needs. If your resume doesn’t show it, you’re out.

  • Dozens of versions, each with different numbers

    Over the years you pile up resumes with different dates, titles and results. Recruiters check your resume against LinkedIn — and spot the mismatches.

  • Tailoring takes forever

    Rewriting your resume and writing a cover letter for each job by hand takes hours. So most people send the same file everywhere.

We don’t make things up

The AI changes wording, order and emphasis — never the facts. Every line of your resume is backed by your master resume, and a separate check verifies every date and number before you get it. A recruiter can compare your resume with LinkedIn and your references, and everything will line up.

  • No contradictions in numbers or facts
  • Your key achievements and most relevant experience up front
  • A clear answer on whether the job fits you — and whether you fit the job

Your resume in other languages — no translator needed

A word-for-word translation isn’t enough: a recruiter in Berlin or Madrid expects a different structure, local job titles, and familiar date and number formats. We write your resume and cover letter in another language following local conventions — from the same facts as your English version.

  • Not a translation — a local resume

    Structure and phrasing the way resumes are written in that country, with the sections local recruiters expect.

  • Local job titles

    “Leiterin E-Commerce” in Germany, “Responsable de E-commerce” in Spain — the titles local recruiters search for, confirmed with you.

  • Same facts, local formats

    Amounts and dates written the local way (130 Mio. USD, mar. 2021). Nothing that isn’t in your master resume.

  • Cover letter, too

    A full version for email and a short one for LinkedIn or a messaging app — in the language of the posting.
